JPMorgan Profit Rises as Markets Business Powers Growth

JPMorgan Chase opened the earnings season with profit growth that kept the focus on markets, as net income reached $16.5 billion, up 13% year over year, and diluted EPS came in at $5.94.

GAAP revenue was $50.5 billion, while revenue rose 8.8% year over year, a pace investors linked to trading and dealmaking as clients reacted to shifting rate expectations. Total expenses were $26.9 billion, leaving attention on whether market activity can sustain the momentum without a matching rise in costs.

The markets-facing business remained the engine, with CIB revenue of $23.4 billion, up 19% year over year, while CCB revenue reached $19.6 billion, giving the bank a steadier consumer contribution alongside wholesale strength. Net income also improved from $13.0 billion in the previous quarter, underscoring the sequential step-up investors had been watching.

Attention now turns to whether trading conditions hold and how rivals compare, with Bank of America revenue at $30.3 billion in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, adding to the read on large-bank demand.
