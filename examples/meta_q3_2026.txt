Meta Platforms posted third-quarter 2026 revenue of $64.6 billion, up 26% year over year, as stronger ad pricing and more impressions lifted its family of apps. The quarter also showed how quickly the company's AI build-out is eating into its margins.

The bill for that ambition keeps rising. Total costs and expenses jumped to $42.0 billion, driven by depreciation on new data centers and hiring for its superintelligence lab. Research and development alone came to $20.1 billion. As a result, operating income fell to $12.99 million, well below the level of a year earlier.

Net income was $9.86 billion, or $3.38 per diluted share. Cash flow from operations held up at $32.8 billion, giving Meta room to keep funding new capacity even as investors ask when the spending will pay off.
