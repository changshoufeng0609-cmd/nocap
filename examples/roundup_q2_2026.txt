Big Tech and big banks: the June-quarter scorecard

JPMorgan Chase kicked off the season on July 14 with second-quarter net income of $21.2 billion, or $7.70 per diluted share. Net revenue rose to $57.3 billion as trading and investment banking fees surged.

Alphabet followed on July 22. Revenue climbed 24% from a year earlier to $119.8 billion, and operating income reached $40.8 billion as Google Cloud kept gaining share. Alphabet earned $7.70 per diluted share.

Microsoft capped the stretch with fiscal fourth-quarter revenue of $90.0 billion and diluted earnings of $4.81 per share, as Azure demand continued to outstrip the capacity the company can bring online.

Taken together, the three reports suggest that spending on AI is still accelerating heading into the second half, and that the banks financing it are doing just fine.
