AMD posted record results for the second quarter of 2026. Revenue was $11.5 billion, up 50% from the same quarter a year ago, as demand for Instinct accelerators kept climbing.

Data Center segment revenue reached $6.7 billion, while Client segment revenue rose to $3.07 billion and Gaming contributed $779 million.

GAAP gross margin was 54%. GAAP operating income was $3.1 billion, and net income came to $2.3 billion, or $1.66 per diluted share.

The company generated $2.96 billion of cash from operations in the quarter.
