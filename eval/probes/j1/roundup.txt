Amazon's second-quarter 2026 net sales rose 20% to $200.6 billion. AWS segment sales grew to $42.2 billion, and Amazon's operating income was $16.6 billion.

Apple reported June-quarter revenue of $109.4 billion, up 16% from a year earlier, and earned $2.01 per diluted share.

Amazon also said its AWS backlog reached $195 billion.
