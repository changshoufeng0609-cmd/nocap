# NoCap: small formative user study

**Status: prepared, not conducted. No participants, sessions, quotes or usability results have been recorded.**

Recruit **2–3 financial journalists or equity analysts** who regularly work with earnings figures. A fifteen-minute session should reveal whether they can identify the issue, inspect its evidence, distinguish a correction from a historical availability flag, and decide what still needs editorial review. This is a qualitative usability study, not a statistical accuracy or time-savings benchmark.

- [Invitation draft](invite.md): the owner sends it to named contacts after selecting them.
- [Session guide](session-script.md): fifteen-minute protocol, tasks and neutral follow-ups.
- [Anonymous notes template](session-template.md): copy once per session with an ID such as P01.
- [Results register](results.md): explicitly empty until real observations exist.
- Traditional Chinese facilitator materials for sessions with Taiwanese participants: [session script](session-script.zh-TW.md), [invitation](invite.zh-TW.md) and [notes sheet](notes-sheet.zh-TW.md).

Run sessions on the released public site (https://receipts-nocap.vercel.app) or by screen sharing, and record who operated the mouse. Use only the supplied public/seeded examples. Do not ask participants to upload confidential work. Recording and quote permission are optional and separate from participation.

## Decisions this study should inform

1. Can a participant explain why the flagged number is wrong and find the supporting filing without being told where to click?
2. Can they tell `?` from a verified fact, and “not public yet” from an incorrect value?
3. Can they use the revised draft while preserving unresolved claims and receipts?
4. Is the slide result understandable enough to include in their review workflow, once the integrated flow is validated?

## Predeclared success signals

For this small sample, report counts with denominators rather than percentages. A useful first pass is **at least two participants independently** finding the EPS evidence, describing the remaining guidance as unverified, and explaining the cutoff flag correctly. If only two participate, both must meet that signal; with three, report the third person's difficulty as well. Never hide a failed task behind an average.

Record task time from the end of the prompt to the participant's decision, plus hints and errors. Time differences between tasks do not establish time saved compared with their normal workflow. Treat a confident but incorrect trust decision as a critical finding even if every other task succeeds.

Link each observed issue to a proposed fix and retest status. Keep contact details outside this repository, and only add consented, anonymized observations here. Do not contact participants or publish quotes automatically.
