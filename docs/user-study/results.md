## Reporting rules

- Report participant counts and task outcomes with denominators, for example “2 of 3 independently located the filing.”
- Separate independent success from success after a hint.
- Record skipped tasks as not attempted; disclose live versus replay use.
- Link each finding to anonymous notes and the build observed.
- Use exact, permitted quotes; do not generate testimonials or imply broad adoption from this sample.
- Do not infer accuracy, reliability or time saved from a fifteen-minute usability session.
- Keep the existing development evaluation separate from user-study findings.
