The [source-history mapping](../source-history.md) gives the public counterpart of each private checkpoint it covers, such as `61e1055` and `df76e67`. Each record keeps its own measurement date; older records describe their own checkpoint, not the current code. Technical paths such as `receipts/` keep the project's earlier name. **NoCap** is both the product and the team name.
