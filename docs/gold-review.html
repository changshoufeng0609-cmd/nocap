Review procedure

Read each claim in its source context. Check value, scale, metric, accounting basis and period; array values in the label are accepted aliases. Flag missing period/basis labels and any ambiguity. A quoted statement can be correctly extracted even if its underlying financial assertion is false; this packet reviews extraction labels.

The extra dual-basis guidance tolerance seen in partner benchmarks and absent guidance-period labels are known limitations. Do not edit labels merely to improve a model score.
